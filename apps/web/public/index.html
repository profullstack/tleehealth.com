GET PAID FOR COORDINATION

Medicare pays for the help your staff already gives.

Chasing a prior authorization. Booking the oncologist. Finding a ride to infusion. Since 2024 Medicare pays practices for this work, roughly $75 to $100 a month per patient for the first hour, depending on where you practice. Most practices never bill it, because nobody counts the minutes.

  1. 01Your advocate logs the time. A staff member helps a patient, picks what they did and how long it took. The care plan keeps the goals and who owes what.
  2. 02The month adds itself up. Minutes turn into billing codes per patient, and the caseload shows how close each one is to the next.
  3. 03Hand your biller a superbill. One spreadsheet of what to claim, with anything that would get a claim rejected (missing consent, no qualifying visit, no NPI) held back and named.
